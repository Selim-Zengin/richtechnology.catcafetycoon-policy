Cat Cafe Tycoon – Privacy Policy

Developer: Rich Technology · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Cat Cafe Tycoon ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location or any account. The Game has no sign-in and no chat. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive information that directly identifies you.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, gems, level, cafes, upgrades), your settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and similar game data are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the Game or its data removes it permanently.

3. Game statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics. Firebase may automatically collect information such as:

  • an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation) and, where permitted, your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • approximate (country/region level) location derived from your IP address,
  • usage information, for example when the Game is first opened, session length, when it is uninstalled, and game events such as reaching a new level, finishing the tutorial, opening a new area or watching a rewarded ad.

We only see this information in aggregated, statistical form; it does not tell us who you are. Google processes this data on our behalf according to the Firebase Privacy and Security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads (banner ads and optional rewarded video ads) provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and may be used to show personalized ads where permitted. Google's practices are described here: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) when the Game starts. Advertising and statistics only start after this choice. You can change your choice later from the Game's settings (Privacy button).
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching rewarded ads is always optional.

6. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game may send local reminder notifications (for example, a daily reward reminder). These are created on your device; no personal data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

7. Updates

The Game uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a required new version is available. This check is handled by Google Play on your device.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our own systems. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Firebase Analytics is stored and retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "Last updated" date above.
